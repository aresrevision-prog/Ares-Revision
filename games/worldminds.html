Up to ten AIs guide rival nations from the first humans to the stars and on into three galaxies full of alien civilisations. Rebels found new nations along the way. They farm, trade, ally, betray, go to war, and maybe one day unite.

Each square is 1 km². Click or scroll out to return to the solar system. Land can only be claimed within 7 km of a colony or mine, so nations build outward one settlement at a time. A people must hold 20 km² of a world before its rockets can push on to the next one, so crowded worlds lead to war.
